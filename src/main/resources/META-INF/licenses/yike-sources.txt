MYRIAD original Yike / Prismatic Array assets, 2026-11-13.

Weapon meshes and painted texture atlas:
  Authoring source: art_source/yike/hong_lie*.spec.json and
    tools/assets/generate_yike_weapon.py
  Editable Blockbench parts: art_source/yike/hong_lie*.bbmodel
  Texture source: art_source/yike/hong_lie_atlas.png
  Original cuboid geometry and deterministic palette/engraving, created for this project.
  Embedded atlas, face-bound materials and connected focusing rings were refined
  for the static-asset pass. Previews are rendered from the actual model parts.
  No third-party weapon mesh, texture, icon or audio sample is included.

Concept reference:
  art_source/yike/hong_lie_concept.png
  Generated with the built-in image generation tool from the project's confirmed
  original three-petal, dark-violet/coral/pale-gold arcane-staff brief.
  The complete prompt is retained in art_source/yike/README.md.
  The concept image is an authoring reference, not a game texture or model.

Skill icons, visual geometry, sounds:
  Icons: eight new candidates generated with the built-in image generation tool
    from the confirmed original skill descriptions and palette. Source sheet:
    art_source/yike/icon_source.png. Measured individual panels are cropped and
    exported as opaque 64x64 PNGs, replacing the earlier geometric drawings.
  VFX: YikeVfxGeometry.java, original faceted crystals, open petals and ribbons.
  Audio: original oscillator/noise synthesis in the generator, then mono
  Ogg Vorbis encoding with libsndfile via soundfile. No sampled audio, voices,
  musical recordings, commercial libraries or other game's sound effects.
  Three independent short shot variants plus deployment, impact, warning,
  barrier, heavy and beam stages. The beam loop is a periodic authored waveform.
