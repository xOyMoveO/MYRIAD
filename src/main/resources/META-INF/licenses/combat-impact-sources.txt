Derived assets: assets/myriad/sounds/combat_impact/*.ogg
Changes: mono 48kHz conversion, start-silence trimming, low-frequency cleanup,
short tails/fades, level balancing, and layering existing Foley recordings.
No music, voice, franchise sounds, or synthesized electronic tones are used.

Blade/critical/swing: knifeSlice, knifeSlice2, cloth1..3 (RPG Audio).
Blade/blunt/arcane/critical: impactPunch_medium/heavy_000..002.
Projectile: impactSoft_heavy_000..002, impactWood_light_000..002.
Shield/arcane: impactGlass_light/medium_000..002.
Blade/critical/structure/shield: impactMetal_light/medium/heavy_000..002.
Blunt/structure: impactWood_heavy_000..002; structure: impactMining_000..002.
Shield: impactSoft_medium_000..002.
